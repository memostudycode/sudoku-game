Sudoku

A little focus, one square at a time.

Quick guide

Fill the grid so every row, column, and 3×3 box contains the numbers 1–9 exactly once.

Getting started

  • Choose a challengePick a difficulty at the top to start a fresh puzzle.
  • Select a squareTap a square to highlight its row, column, and box.
  • Enter a numberChoose a number button to fill the selected square.

Button guide

  • PencilToggle note mode to jot down possible numbers.
  • HintFill the selected empty square with its answer (limited uses).
  • UndoUndo your most recent number entry.
  • CheckHighlight squares that break Sudoku rules.
  • SubmitCheck your completed grid.
  • Clear (×)Remove your entry from the selected square.
  • Time modeStart and pause the timer whenever you like.
